# First External Beta Tester Instructions

> **DRAFT — testing has not started.** Use only after pre-invite GO, private invitation, and recorded consent.

This is invite-only product QA, not public or clinical use. PhysioVision AI does not diagnose, prescribe treatment, or replace a licensed healthcare professional. Feedback can be inaccurate.

1. Read the approved consent/privacy notice and known limitations.
2. Install the approved internal Android build from the private link.
3. Open the app and confirm the safety disclaimer is visible.
4. Create a demo account using a non-identifying alias and beta-only email details provided by the coordinator.
5. Choose only the supported exercise assigned to you.
6. Follow the exercise-specific camera guidance and keep required joints visible.
7. Upload or record only an approved short test video.
8. Review success/rejected status, score handling, feedback, confidence, disclaimer, and limitations carefully.
9. Try one approved invalid/static video and confirm rejection shows no movement score or ML result.
10. Complete one safe retry or network-interruption check if assigned.
11. Submit the private feedback form and report bugs or safety/privacy concerns through the assigned channels.

**Do not upload real patient videos or identifiable personal information.** Do not enter diagnosis, symptoms, treatment history, names, record numbers, or raw credentials in forms or issue reports.

Stop immediately for pain, dizziness, numbness, instability, unusual discomfort, chest symptoms, or unusual shortness of breath and seek appropriate professional or urgent review. Withdraw at any time by notifying support and requesting access revocation/deletion through the approved private channel.
