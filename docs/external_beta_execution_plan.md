## Execution status and safety boundary

This plan is for an invite-only beta only; it is not a public release. Use non-identifying test videos only—no real patient data or sensitive health information. It is not for clinical use, diagnosis, treatment, or medical decisions. Execution has not started and is blocked until private staging, an installed Android build, physical-device QA, active feedback/support/privacy links, and accountable approval are ready.

## Entry sequence

1. Multi-owner GO decision and verified private links.
2. Alias-only roster entry with invitation status.
3. Versioned consent and all safety/data acknowledgements.
4. Minimum two-exercise assignment plus safe negative/network case.
5. Staffed support window and active pause/revocation capability.
6. Daily monitoring report and restricted incident review.
7. Closeout, cleanup/deletion review, and execution report.

Stop immediately for suspected privacy exposure, real patient-identifiable content, unsafe/diagnostic wording, cross-user access, public artifacts, repeated launch crashes, majority upload failure, serious token/session flaw, or safety misunderstanding. Do not resume until containment, verification, and a new decision are recorded.
