# External Beta Pause and Stop Protocol

> Invite-only beta only. No public release. No real patient data. No clinical use. No diagnosis or treatment claims. The beta is blocked until private staging, the Android build, physical-device QA, and active feedback/support links are ready.

## Boundary

This protocol governs a future invite-only beta, never a public release or clinical service. No real patient data, diagnosis, treatment, or medical decision-making is allowed. The beta is currently blocked before start by staging, Android build, physical QA, and inactive support/feedback/privacy links.

Pause immediately for a suspected privacy/security incident, real patient-identifiable upload, artifact exposure, cross-user session ownership, tester-facing diagnostic/clinical wording, repeated launch crash, majority upload failure, serious auth/token defect, or safety misunderstanding.

## Authority and response

The staffed beta lead may pause immediately; security/privacy and safety owners confirm scope, while the release owner controls resume. A critical report does not wait for consensus.

1. Stop invitations/uploads and post a short private “stop testing” notice.
2. Revoke affected accounts, tokens, build links, artifacts, or environment access using the documented admin process.
3. Isolate minimum evidence; never forward identifiable material through ordinary chat/issues.
4. Identify and delete inappropriate uploads/artifacts when authorized; preserve only required incident evidence.
5. Record time, reporter alias, category, affected systems, containment, access/deletion actions, owner, and next update in the restricted incident record.
6. Notify testers through `[PRIVATE STATUS CHANNEL]` without revealing another tester or speculative detail.
7. Verify remediation with synthetic/non-identifying fixtures and repeat security/device tests.
8. Resume only after privacy/security, safety, QA, and release owners record a new GO decision.

A tester may also withdraw independently; revoke their access, stop assignments, process eligible deletion, and update status without coercion.
