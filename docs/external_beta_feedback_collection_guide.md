# External Beta Feedback Collection Guide

> Invite-only beta only. No public release. No real patient data. No clinical use. No diagnosis or treatment claims. The beta is blocked until private staging, the Android build, physical-device QA, and active feedback/support links are ready.

## Boundary

Feedback is for invite-only product QA only, not a public release or clinical study. Do not collect real patient data, health history, diagnosis, symptoms, treatment, outcomes, or medical advice. Collection remains blocked until staging/build/device QA and private feedback/support/privacy links are active.

Use `external_beta_feedback_template.csv` or the approved private form. Capture beta alias, device/platform/build/environment, exercise, video source category, upload status, analysis status, rejected-result clarity, result/confidence/camera-guidance clarity, disclaimer visibility, limitation understanding, technical bugs, safety/privacy concern flag, and product suggestion.

Do not ask whether the analysis diagnosed an injury, improved health, prescribed appropriate treatment, or matched a clinical assessment. Do not attach media by default. If restricted evidence is essential, confirm the file is a permitted non-identifying test fixture, use the approved private channel, restrict access, and apply retention/deletion rules.

Safety/privacy flags bypass ordinary feedback triage: stop testing and contact `[PRIVATE INCIDENT CONTACT]`. Ordinary feedback goes to `[PRIVATE FEEDBACK LINK]`; bugs go to `[PRIVATE ISSUE LINK]`. Empty feedback means no data collected—not successful testing or validation.
