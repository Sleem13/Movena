## Purpose and scope

The first wave is a private product-QA exercise intended to collect genuine evidence about installation, upload reliability, safe invalid-input rejection, result clarity, guidance, limitations, and support workflows. Target size is 3–5 trusted adult, non-clinical testers. Actual invited count is 0.

Real patient data, patient-identifiable video, health information, clinical use, diagnosis, treatment prescription, and clinical validation claims are prohibited. This is not a public launch or public app-store release.

## Success criteria

- A real alias is privately invited after GO/CONDITIONAL GO.
- Required consent acknowledgements are recorded before assignment.
- Each tester completes at least two supported exercises, an invalid/static case, and a safe retry/network case.
- Genuine feedback/issues are recorded with privacy-safe evidence.
- Rejected inputs never show a fake score or applicable ML result.
- No unresolved safety/privacy, blocker, or high-severity gate remains.

## Stop criteria

Pause immediately for real patient-identifiable data, a privacy/security incident, diagnostic wording, public/cross-user artifact exposure, a fake rejected score, launch crashes, uploads failing for most testers, or unsafe interpretation. Restrict evidence and notify the accountable privacy/safety owner.
