# External Beta Issue Intake Protocol

> Invite-only beta only. No public release. No real patient data. No clinical use. No diagnosis or treatment claims. The beta is blocked until private staging, the Android build, physical-device QA, and active feedback/support links are ready.

## Boundary

Issue intake supports an invite-only beta only, not public or clinical use. Never request real patient media, identifying data, diagnosis, treatment details, raw tokens, secrets, or signed URLs. Execution remains blocked until staging, Android build, physical QA, and active private issue/support/privacy paths exist.

Testers report through `[PRIVATE ISSUE LINK]` using alias, platform/device/OS, app version, environment, exercise, category, steps, expected behavior, actual behavior, and whether privacy-safe logs/screenshots are available. Do not paste restricted evidence into the general tracker.

Severity:

- **Safety/privacy critical:** suspected exposure, real patient-identifiable upload, cross-user data, public artifact, or unsafe claim.
- **Blocker:** launch/login/core testing cannot continue safely.
- **High:** major upload/result/auth/artifact failure affecting core testing.
- **Medium:** material issue with a safe workaround.
- **Low:** minor usability, copy, or documentation issue.

Critical examples include patient-identifiable upload, exposed sensitive artifact, diagnosis-implying wording, a form asking for clinical information, or private data visible to the wrong user. Pause immediately, use `[PRIVATE INCIDENT CONTACT]`, revoke affected access, preserve minimum safe evidence, and notify the privacy/security and safety owners. Target acknowledgement: critical within one staffed hour, blocker within four staffed hours, high within one business day. Resume requires verified remediation and a new decision.
