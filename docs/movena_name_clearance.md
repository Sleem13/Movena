# Movena Name Clearance Memo

Date: 2026-09-08

Status: preliminary clearance only. Do not treat this memo as legal advice or a trademark opinion.

## Proposed mark

- Product name: Movena
- Positioning: movement intelligence for therapist-guided recovery
- Tagline: Move better. Recover together.
- Intended field: software for camera-assisted exercise monitoring, therapist-guided rehabilitation workflows, movement reports, recovery coaching, and clinician review.

## Public-use recommendation

Do not commit, publish, announce, submit to app stores, buy ads, or file public-facing materials under Movena until a trademark attorney completes a formal clearance search and the team reserves an acceptable domain/app-store handle set.

Movena can remain a local working name while clearance is pending, but the name has meaningful conflicts in adjacent "movement" and software spaces.

## Formal clearance required

Ask trademark counsel to review at least:

- Exact and confusingly similar marks: Movena, Movenna, Movina, Moveena, MoveNow, MoveWell, Move AI, Move Health, Move Rehab, Move Recovery, and phonetic equivalents.
- U.S. federal marks through USPTO, including live/dead records and goods/services in classes 9, 10, 35, 41, 42, and 44.
- International marks through WIPO, EUIPO/TMview, UKIPO, DPMA, and target launch markets.
- Common-law use: websites, app stores, social media handles, health-software directories, fitness products, clinical software, rehabilitation tools, and AI movement-analysis tools.
- Domain and app-store availability for the exact name and the planned launch geography.

## Safer launch posture while pending

- Use "Movena" only as an internal/local code and design name until counsel clears it.
- Do not use `TM`, registered-trademark symbols, or "official" claims.
- Avoid claims that imply diagnosis, autonomous treatment, clinical validation, or medical-device approval.
- Prefer descriptive copy: "supports exercise monitoring" and "therapist-guided recovery" over stronger clinical efficacy claims.
- Keep disclaimers visible: Movena does not diagnose, prescribe treatment, or replace assessment by a licensed professional.

## Decision

Preliminary recommendation: hold public launch under Movena until legal review resolves the exact-name software conflict, the fitness-product conflict, and domain crowding.
