# Internal Tester Onboarding

1. Confirm you are an approved internal tester and will use no patient or identifying data.
2. Read the known limitations, safety/consent text, camera guidance, and issue process.
3. Install only the recorded internal build from the approved EAS link.
4. Use the assigned staging account; never share credentials or install links.
5. Record device model, OS, app version, exercise, and non-identifying test scenario.
6. Stop for pain, dizziness, unusual symptoms, or unsafe conditions. Do not use results for diagnosis or treatment.
7. Report safety/privacy issues immediately and avoid attaching raw video unless specifically approved through a secure channel.
8. Log out after testing and delete local test recordings if they are no longer needed.

Access may be revoked at any time. Staging results and reports are temporary educational engineering outputs, not health records.
