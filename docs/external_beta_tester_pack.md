## Welcome

Thank you for helping test Movena. This private, invite-only beta checks whether the app installs, uploads controlled exercise videos, explains results, and handles errors clearly. It is product QA, not clinical testing.

Movena supports exercise monitoring and does not replace assessment by a licensed physiotherapist. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose injury or disease, and does not prescribe treatment. Results may be inaccurate.

## Before testing

- Read and accept `[CONSENT_PRIVACY_URL]` and `[KNOWN_LIMITATIONS_URL]`.
- **Do not upload videos containing real patients, identifiable personal information, or sensitive health information.**
- Use your own non-identifying test recording or a supplied fixture. Do not use the app for pain, emergency symptoms, or a medical decision.
- Install only from `[PRIVATE_ANDROID_INSTALL_URL]`; do not share the package or credentials.

## Supported exercises and recording tips

Test only bodyweight squat, sit-to-stand, knee extension, shoulder abduction, and hip abduction. Select the matching exercise. Follow its camera guide; keep required joints visible, use a stable camera and good light, avoid clutter/occlusion, and record a short controlled set.

## Suggested tasks

1. Install and sign in with `[BETA LOGIN INSTRUCTIONS]`.
2. Open the library and camera guide.

Known limitations include camera-dependent pose estimation, possible rejection/inaccuracy, non-clinical scores, temporary/expiring artifacts, network/backend dependency, five exercises only, and experimental ML/DL where present.
