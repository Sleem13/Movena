# External Tester Onboarding

> Draft only. Do not send until the go/no-go owner records `GO` and supplies real install, login, feedback, and support links.

## Before you start

PhysioVision AI is an experimental movement-monitoring app. It describes observed movement patterns from a test video. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose injury or disease, does not prescribe treatment, and does not replace a licensed physiotherapist.

**Do not upload videos containing real patients, identifiable personal information, or sensitive health information.** Use only a video of yourself created for this beta, or a supplied test video. Stop exercising if you feel pain, dizziness, instability, or another concerning symptom and seek appropriate professional help.

## Join and test

1. Read the consent/privacy text and known limitations; participate only if you agree.
2. Install the private beta from `[INSTALL LINK — REQUIRED BEFORE GO]`. Do not share the link or app package.
3. Connect only to `[PRIVATE STAGING URL — REQUIRED BEFORE GO]`.
4. Sign in with the beta account or code sent directly to you at `[BETA LOGIN INSTRUCTIONS]`; do not reuse a sensitive password.
5. Choose one of the five supported exercises. Do not use another exercise and expect a meaningful result.
6. Follow the in-app camera guide. Keep the full relevant body area visible, use stable framing and good light, and record a short controlled test.
7. Check the frame for names, screens, documents, faces of bystanders, or other identifiers before uploading.
8. Upload once. If it fails, record the error, network type, and retry behavior without copying tokens or secrets.
9. Read the status, confidence, limitations, and feedback together. A rejected result means the recording, visibility, or movement was insufficient; it is not a clinical finding. Scores are not clinical scores.
10. Submit product feedback at `[FEEDBACK FORM LINK]`; report bugs through `[BUG REPORT LINK]`. Use your beta alias, not your real name or health history.

## Reporting and leaving

Report privacy or safety concerns immediately to `[BETA SUPPORT CONTACT]` and stop using the app until instructed. For ordinary bugs, include exercise, app version, device/OS, expected and actual behavior, and reproduction steps. Share screenshots only after removing personal information; do not share videos unless the beta team explicitly confirms a privacy-safe channel and the video meets the test-data rules.

You may leave at any time: uninstall the app, stop using the beta account, request access revocation, and use `[DELETION REQUEST CONTACT]` for eligible beta data. These placeholders must be replaced and tested before onboarding begins.
