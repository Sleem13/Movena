# Sit-to-Stand Safety and Product Notes

- Use a stable chair on a secure surface.
- Place the phone on a stable surface and prefer a side or oblique view.
- Keep the body and chair visible, especially shoulders, hips, knees, and ankles.
- Record three to five comfortable repetitions when appropriate.
- Stop if pain, dizziness, loss of balance, or unusual discomfort occurs and seek appropriate professional guidance.

PhysioVision AI provides exercise-monitoring and educational support. It does not assess fall risk, diagnose frailty, identify neurological or orthopedic disease, prescribe treatment, or replace assessment by a licensed physiotherapist or healthcare professional.

The score is a rule-based recording summary, not a clinical outcome measure. Chair detection, hand-support detection, load measurement, and validated functional-test timing are outside Sprint 9.
