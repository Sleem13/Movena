# External Beta Known Limitations — Short Version

PhysioVision AI is an invite-only experimental beta candidate, not a public release or medical device. It supports only five listed exercises. Camera position, lighting, clothing, occlusion, device/network conditions, and pose-estimation errors can change or prevent results. A rejected result means the video was not sufficient for analysis; it is not a clinical finding. Scores are not clinical scores. ML/DL, when shown, is experimental. Do not use the app for diagnosis, treatment prescription, emergencies, or medical decisions, and do not upload real patients or identifying/sensitive health information. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness, or unusual discomfort and seek appropriate professional review. PhysioVision AI does not replace assessment by a licensed physiotherapist.
