# Therapist Dashboard Privacy and Safety Notes

The dashboard is a development prototype with no login, role enforcement, tenant isolation, or privacy boundary. Anyone with API access can view or modify local placeholder profiles.

- Do not use real patient data.
- Do not deploy the dashboard as a clinical record system.
- Do not interpret scores or issue counts as diagnoses, prognosis, fall risk, or treatment recommendations.
- AI feedback supports exercise monitoring and does not replace licensed physiotherapist assessment.

Production prerequisites include authentication, role-based permissions, consent, encryption in transit and at rest, secure media storage, audit logs, backup and retention controls, data-subject deletion workflows, privacy policy, threat modeling, and jurisdiction-specific compliance review.
